# User Guide: How to Register as a Voter

## Overview

This guide will walk you through the complete voter registration process on the Smart Voting system

## Prerequisites

Before you begin, ensure you have:

- A valid Aadhaar card (12-digit number)
- A Gmail address
- A 10-digit mobile phone number
- a digital passport size photo (Accepted file formats JPG, JPEG and PNG)

## Step-by-Step Registration Process

### Step 1: Access the Registration Portal

1. Go to the homepage/Dashboard 
2. Click the **"New Registration"** button 
3. You will be redirected to the E-Enrollment page

### Step 2: OTP Verification

1. Enter your mobile number(10 digits)
2. Click **"Send OTP"** button
3. An OTP will be sent to your mobile number via SMS
4. Enter the 6-digit OTP in the verification field
5. Click **"Verify OTP"**
6. Wait for confirmation message

**OTP Details:**
- Valid for 10 minutes
- Can be requested again after 1 minute
- Maximum 3 attempts per hour

### Step 3: Aadhaar number

1. Enter your mobile number(12 digits)

### Step 4: Enter Personal Information

Fill in the following required fields:

- **Full Name**: Enter your complete name as per Aadhaar
- **Age**: Must be 18 years or older
- **Gender**: Select Male, Female, or Other
- **Email**: Must be a valid Gmail address (@gmail.com)
- **Mobile Number**: Enter your 10-digit phone number (no country code)

**Important Notes:**
- All fields are mandatory
- Email must end with @gmail.com
- Phone number must be exactly 10 digits

### Step 4: Upload Photo

1. Click **"Upload file"** 
2. Choose your file to upload

**Photo Requirements:**
- Should be a passport size photo
- Face must be clearly visible
- No sunglasses or face coverings
- Neutral expression recommended

### Step 5: Submit Registration

1. Review all entered information
2. Ensure photo is clear and acceptable
3. Click **"Register Voter"** button
4. Wait for processing (may take 10-30 seconds)

### Step 6: Receive Voter ID

Upon successful registration:

1. This ID will be displayed on screen
2. A confirmation will be sent to your email


## Common Issues

**"Aadhaar already registered"**
- Each Aadhaar can only register once
- One person = one vote
- Contact support for assistance

**"OTP not received"**
- Check your phone number is correct
- Wait 1-2 minutes for SMS delivery
- Check spam/blocked messages
- Request OTP again

## After Registration

Once registered, you can:

1. **Verify Registration**: Use "Search E-Roll" to confirm your details
2. **EPIC download**: Use "Download EPIC" to download your Voter ID

## Need Help?

If you encounter any issues:

- Check the FAQ section
- Contact election support
- Visit the help center
- Email: support@smartvote.gov

**Remember**: Your Voter ID is your key to voting. Keep it secure and accessible for election day!
